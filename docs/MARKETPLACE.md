# 🛒 Marketplace Guide

## Overview

The Unified Agricultural Marketplace is a comprehensive digital platform that connects farmers, sellers, and buyers in a seamless agricultural ecosystem. It supports both B2B (Business-to-Business) and B2C (Business-to-Consumer) transactions with advanced features for product management, seller verification, and quality assurance.

### 👥 Seller Management

## Product Quality Standards

### 🌟 Grade Classifications

#### Grade A+ (Premium)
- **Purity**: >99%
- **Moisture**: Optimal levels (varies by product)
- **Appearance**: Uniform size, color, and texture
- **Contamination**: Zero foreign matter
- **Certification**: Organic or premium quality certificates

#### Grade A (Excellent)
- **Purity**: 95-99%
- **Moisture**: Within acceptable limits
- **Appearance**: Good uniformity with minor variations
- **Contamination**: Minimal foreign matter (<0.5%)
- **Quality**: Meets all standard quality parameters

#### Grade B+ (Good)
- **Purity**: 90-95%
- **Moisture**: Slightly above optimal but acceptable
- **Appearance**: Some variation in size/color
- **Contamination**: Minor foreign matter (<1%)
- **Quality**: Good quality for general consumption

#### Grade B (Standard)
- **Purity**: 85-90%
- **Moisture**: Higher than optimal but safe
- **Appearance**: Noticeable variations
- **Contamination**: Foreign matter (<2%)
- **Quality**: Suitable for processing or bulk use

### 🧪 Quality Testing Parameters

#### Physical Parameters
- **Size Distribution**: Grain size consistency
- **Color Analysis**: Natural color retention
- **Texture Assessment**: Firmness and integrity
- **Weight Measurement**: Density and specific weight

#### Chemical Parameters
- **Moisture Content**: Water content percentage
- **Protein Content**: Nutritional value assessment
- **Pesticide Residue**: Safety compliance testing
- **Heavy Metals**: Lead, mercury, cadmium levels

#### Microbiological Parameters
- **Bacterial Count**: Total aerobic bacteria
- **Fungal Testing**: Mold and yeast presence
- **Pathogen Detection**: E.coli, Salmonella testing
- **Shelf Life**: Storage stability assessment

- Browse categories or search products
- Apply filters (price, location, quality, organic)

#### 2. Order Placement
- Select quantity and specifications
