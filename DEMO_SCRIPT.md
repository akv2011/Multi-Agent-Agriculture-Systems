## Q&A Handling Guide

### Expected Questions & Prepared Answers:

**Q: "How do you ensure satellite data accuracy?"**
**A:** *"We use multiple satellite sources (Sentinel-2, Landsat-8, MODIS) with cross-validation. Our confidence scoring system ranges from 0-1, and we only make recommendations above 0.7 confidence. We also have ground truth validation from farmer feedback."*

**Q: "What happens if satellite data is unavailable due to clouds?"**
**A:** *"Our system uses temporal interpolation and caches recent data. We have 5-day average fallbacks and can operate with 85% accuracy even without real-time satellite data. Cloud cover is factored into our confidence scoring."*

**Q: "How do you compete with existing AgTech companies?"**
**A:** *"No one else integrates real-time satellite data with multi-agent AI systems. Companies like DeHaat provide information, but we provide intelligence. Our satellite integration creates a defensive moat that's extremely difficult to replicate."*

**Q: "What's your customer acquisition cost?"**
**A:** *"For farmers, our CAC is ₹500 through field demonstrations and word-of-mouth. For businesses, it's ₹15,000 but with much higher lifetime value. Our network effects reduce CAC over time as users refer others."*

**Q: "How do you monetize the platform?"**
**A:** *"Three revenue streams: 2-3% marketplace commission (primary), AI-as-a-Service subscriptions (secondary), and business intelligence reports (highest margin). We project ₹25 crore revenue by Year 2."*

**Q: "What's your regulatory risk?"**
**A:** *"We're aligned with government policy promoting digital agriculture. We're already in discussions with PM-KISAN for integration. Our technology supports government goals of increasing farmer income and reducing input costs."*
